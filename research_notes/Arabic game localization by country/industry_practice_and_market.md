# Arabic Game Localization by Country: Industry Practice and Market Context

Research date: 2026-10-13. Every statistic below carries its data year. Most of the market data is from Niko Partners, either reported secondhand or vendor-sourced, so treat it as indicative. No publisher has published a controlled per-dialect A/B result.

## 1. Publisher case studies: MSA vs dialect, single "ar" vs regional variants, measured impact

### Takeaway
Leading global publishers ship one Arabic build in Modern Standard Arabic (MSA), with GCC-targeted events and community management layered on top. PUBG Mobile is the best-documented example. I found no public evidence of a major publisher shipping separate ar-SA and ar-EG in-game string sets. Measured impact data is old and thin: Castle Clash reported +58% MENA revenue within 4 months of its Arabic launch (2016). The most recent signal comes from chart share. Arabic-first titles held 12-22% of the Saudi iOS Top 50 in every quarter from July 2022 to April 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **PUBG Mobile (Level Infinite/Tencent):** Arab News describes it as the most popular mobile game in the region and the leader in player spending. It offers MSA as the in-game language, is "localized for GCC markets with region-specific language and customizations", and has a dedicated regional community management team that gathers linguistic and cultural feedback (article Aug 2024, citing the Niko Partners / Saudi Esports Federation report) — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2567682/amp)
- A commercial Kuwait game guide says PUBG Mobile has full MSA localization, including voiceovers and region-specific events for GCC players. The source is weak (commercial blog, undated) — [Juego Studio](https://www.juegostudio.com/blog/popular-games-in-kuwait)
- **PUBG Mobile and Free Fire (academic):** A 2023 *Open Cultural Studies* study of their Arabic UI text and captions found that localizers mostly used transposition and literal translation. A companion survey of 112 players found that better technical and language quality went with more positive attitudes toward the localization — [De Gruyter, Open Cultural Studies 2023](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/culture-2022-0179/html); [Shoman summary of gamer-reactions study](https://shoman.org/ScienceSociety/ID/4796/video-games-localization-into-arabic-gamers-reactions-to-localizing-pubg-and-free-fire)
- **Castle Clash (IGG):** "When IGG.com launched the Arabic version of Castle Clash, they grew revenue from MENA by 58% within 4 months." The post gives no baseline or methodology (Google Play blog, Nov 2016) — [Android Developers Blog](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2016/11/your-next-growth-market-realizing-the-potential-of-mena.html?hl=en)
- **Wego (travel app, not a game, same Google post, 2016):** After Arabic localization it reported over 200% YoY MENA growth, a rating rise from 3.5 to above 4.5, and +200% retention among Arab users — [Android Developers Blog](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2016/11/your-next-growth-market-realizing-the-potential-of-mena.html?hl=en)
- **Google Play, Saudi Arabia (2016):** 19 of the top 20 grossing apps and games had localized Play Store listings, and most also localized the app itself. Local top-grossing charts were dominated by midcore strategy games, and Clash of Clans, Clash Royale, Mobile Strike and Clash of Kings performed "incredibly well" — [Android Developers Blog](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2016/11/your-next-growth-market-realizing-the-potential-of-mena.html?hl=en)
- **Arabic-first titles on Saudi charts (Antidote, Jul 2022 to Apr 2026, 16 quarterly snapshots):** "Localized" titles, meaning titles with Arabic names or titles from MENA-based studios, held 12-22% of the Saudi iOS Top 50 each quarter but only 0-4% of the Google Play Top 50. In July 2024 they held 50% of the iOS Top 10. Cumulative localized titles in the iOS Top 50 grew from 8 (Jul 2022) to 27 (Apr 2026). Recurring chart publishers were Sawa Game, UMX Studio, Yalla Technology, Abdulrahman Alshimemeri and Remal. TopTop KSA reached #1 in 2025, and Bara Al-Salfa has charted since mid-2022. Common traits were social and multiplayer play, trivia, and traditional board formats. Antidote is a vendor and its definition of "localized" is broad — [Antidote](https://antidote.gg/how-localized-mobile-games-capture-6-10x-more-ios-share-in-saudi-arabia/)
- **Yalla Group (Yalla Ludo):** Yalla Ludo is a casual board-game app built around in-game voice chat and a localized "Majlis" social space. Group revenue was $339.7M in FY2024 and a reported AED 1.256B (~$341.9M) in FY2025. Group MAU was 44.8M in Q4 2025 (+8.2% YoY). Games revenue was $31.2M in Q3 2023, against $53.9M from chat. No per-country split is published — [MENA Startup Digest](https://menastartupdigest.com/?p=32997); [PocketGamer.biz interview](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/our-focus-is-undoubtedly-on-the-middle-east-market-where-we-have-unique-advantages); [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/yalla_group)
- **Yalla Ludo in Iraq:** Zain Iraq runs "YallaPay", a carrier-billed payment portal for Yalla Ludo that works on prepaid lines only — [Zain Iraq](https://www.iq.zain.com/en/yalla-pay)
- **Tamatem (Amman, Jordan):** By April 2018 it had localized about 40 games and translated more than 30,000 lines for *Rage of the Righteous*. Its localization covered characters, music, environments and sometimes storyboards, plus redesigned store icons and previews. For one game it replaced the music with "body sounds" (snapping, humming) because instrumental music was considered religiously unacceptable by part of the Saudi audience. That is a Saudi-specific change, not a pan-Arab one. It also flagged a character named Fatima on a provocative image as a problem. Tamatem says it uses in-house native staff "familiar with regional dialects" and does not outsource to LSPs, though it hires freelancers when needed. I found no published Tamatem dialect policy — [GamesBeat 2018](https://gamesbeat.com/tamatem-has-built-a-thriving-mobile-business-by-bringing-games-to-the-arab-world/); [TechCrunch 2018](https://techcrunch.com/2018/02/01/tamatem/); [Tamatem blog](https://tamatem.co/tag/culture/)
- Tamatem claims Saudi Arabia has "the highest ARPPU in the world: $270". This is self-reported and undated, and I could not verify it — via [LocalizeDirect / search summary](https://www.localizedirect.com/posts/arabic-game-localization)
- **Airport City (via Tamatem, 2018):** Sensor Tower estimated more than 10M downloads since 2014 and over $25M in IAP. These are lifetime global figures, not Arabic-attributable — [GamesBeat 2018](https://gamesbeat.com/tamatem-has-built-a-thriving-mobile-business-by-bringing-games-to-the-arab-world/)
- **Clash of Clans, Coin Master, Monopoly GO, Rovio, Playtika, Miniclip, Netmarble, Free Fire dialect:** I found no official statements on Arabic dialect choice or Arabic localization impact. Clash of Clans is listed with Arabic support only by a third-party directory — [AlternativeTo](https://www.alternativeto.net/software/clash-of-clans/about/)

### Inferences
- The industry default appears to be one MSA Arabic build. Differentiation by country happens in live-ops (GCC events), community management, pricing and marketing, not in separate in-game string tables.
- The Saudi music example shows that cultural content variants can be country-specific even when the language is shared. Content gating by country may matter more than dialect strings.
- Arabic-first social and board games (Yalla Ludo, Sawa, TopTop, Bara Al-Salfa) win with dialect-heavy, voice-chat, culturally native design, not translation. That is a different strategy from global publishers' MSA builds.
- The iOS-vs-Play gap in the Antidote data suggests Arabic-first titles do best on iOS-heavy Gulf audiences. This lines up with the high iOS share in Saudi Arabia and Kuwait (see Section 5).

### Gaps
- I found no public before/after Arabic-localization data on retention, ARPU or ratings from Supercell, Rovio, Playtika, Moon Active, Scopely, Miniclip, Netmarble, Tencent or Garena. Only Castle Clash (2016) gives a number.
- Savvy Games Group's ownership of Scopely: I found nothing on whether it changed Monopoly GO's Arabic approach.
- I could not confirm whether Free Fire or PUBG Mobile use Gulf or Egyptian dialect in any voice lines, events or influencer content.
- The Niko/SEF report reportedly includes PUBG Mobile as a case study, but the full PDF (36 pages, gameindustrylibrary.com) was not readable via fetch.

## 2. App Store and Google Play: per-country store listing mechanics

### Takeaway
On Google Play, custom store listings (CSLs) can be targeted by country. This is the only native way to show, for example, Gulf-dialect copy and screenshots in Saudi Arabia and Egyptian-flavoured assets in Egypt, since both share the single "ar" language listing. On the Apple App Store, "Arabic" is a single metadata locale, and every Arab storefront defaults to English (U.K.) with Arabic as an additional language. Per-country differentiation on iOS has to come from Custom Product Pages, which are reached via URL or Apple Ads and are not organic, and from in-app events with country availability.

### Cited Findings
- **Google Play CSLs:** Up to 50 CSLs, each able to differ in app name, icon, descriptions and graphics. They can target users by country, install state, search keyword or campaign URL. A country can be targeted by only one CSL at a time. Without added translations, a CSL appears in its default language in the targeted countries — [Google Play Console: Custom store listings](https://play.google.com/intl/en-GB_ALL/console/about/customstorelistings); [AppTweak guide](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/custom-store-listings)
- CSLs are the only way to tell apart same-language users in different countries, for example Spanish speakers in Colombia, Mexico and Argentina. Category, contact details and privacy policy stay the same as the main listing — [AppTweak](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/what-are-custom-store-listings-and-how-to-use-them); [Yellowhead](https://www.yellowhead.com/?p=4789)
- An older 2022 source says 5 CSLs. That conflicts with Google's current figure of 50 — [Apptamin 2021/22](https://www.apptamin.com/blog/apples-product-page-customization-wwdc-2021/)
- **Apple App Store locale:** "Arabic" is one supported metadata language, with no ar-SA or ar-EG variants. In Apple's country table, the default language is English (U.K.) with Arabic as an additional language for Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Yemen, Iraq and Jordan. Lebanon and Egypt add French as well. Palestine and Syria do not appear in the table — [Apple App Store Connect Help: App Store localizations](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-store-localizations)
- Arabic metadata support came to App Store Connect in 2019 — [Asodesk 2019](https://asodesk.com/blog/new-languages-in-the-app-store-connect-arabic-and-hebrew/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/amp/story/technology%2Farabic-arrives-on-apples-app-store-1.66528719)
- **Apple Custom Product Pages (CPPs):** Up to 70 extra product page versions (doubled from 35 in Oct 2025, per a third-party guide). Each can vary screenshots, app previews and promotional text, and each is fully localizable. They are distributed via unique URLs and Apple Ads, and can appear in editorial stories and collections. They cannot be reached organically except through curated placements and paid campaigns. Icon, title and subtitle stay the same as the main page — [Apple: Custom product pages](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/custom-product-pages); [AppDrift 2026 guide](https://appdrift.co/blog/app-store-custom-product-pages-guide); [MobileAction](https://www.mobileaction.co/guide/custom-product-pages-guide)
- **Apple in-app events:** Up to 10 published at once and 15 approved per app. Each has its own localizations and a separate Country or Region Availability setting. Name limit is 30 characters, short description 50, long description 120. Events need App Review approval — [Apple: Offer in-app events](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/offer-in-app-events/offer-in-app-events)
- **Google's 2016 MENA advice:** Localize the full store listing (video, screenshots, text), use local dialects for specific countries or formal Arabic for the whole region, and use Store Listing Experiments to optimize — [Android Developers Blog 2016](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2016/11/your-next-growth-market-realizing-the-potential-of-mena.html?hl=en)
- A 2026 market-entry guide advises Arabic store text and screenshots, warning that "a localized build with an English store page leaves most of the conversion benefit unused" (no figure given) — [Game Growth Advisor, May 2026](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-05-28-mena-gaming-market-entry-guide-2026/)
- An academic paper says translated store descriptions with well-chosen keywords can substantially raise downloads (2024, indirect evidence) — [Tandfonline, Cogent Arts & Humanities 2024](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/23311983.2024.2307645)

### Inferences
- A practical setup:
  - **Google Play:** Main "ar" listing in MSA, plus CSLs for Saudi Arabia (Gulf-flavoured copy and screenshots), Egypt (Egyptian tone, lower-price messaging) and possibly Iraq or the Levant. The 50-CSL cap is ample for 13 countries.
  - **iOS:** One MSA Arabic listing, plus CPPs per country, each linked to country-specific Apple Ads and UA campaigns, plus country-limited in-app events for local holidays such as Saudi National Day, UAE National Day and Ramadan.
- On iOS, the default storefront language is English (U.K.), so users whose device language is English see the English listing even in Saudi Arabia. That is a reason to make English-locale screenshots region-aware too. (Inference from Apple's locale table; behaviour not tested.)

### Gaps
- No public case study measures CSL-by-country uplift for Arabic dialect listings.
- I did not confirm whether Apple CPPs can be auto-shown by storefront in search. Sources say they cannot be reached organically except through curated placements and ads.

## 3. In-game per-country variants and country detection

### Takeaway
I found no evidence that shipping ar-SA vs ar-EG in-game strings is common practice. Both industry guidance and player surveys point to MSA for UI and text, with dialect saved for voice and marketing. Country detection for pricing and content is usually storefront-based on iOS (StoreKit Storefront). Apple warns that the storefront can change at any time and should not be stored with customer data.

### Cited Findings
- Guidance for KSA, UAE and Egypt is to use MSA for UI, menus and subtitles, and Gulf or Egyptian dialect only for voice or marketing copy that needs to sound local. It also recommends setting local-currency price tiers (SAR, AED, EGP) rather than auto-converting from USD, with much lower entry price points in Egypt (2026) — [Game Growth Advisor, May 2026](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-05-28-mena-gaming-market-entry-guide-2026/)
- A localization vendor's framework says to use MSA for formal, region-wide content and local dialect for conversational content aimed at a single country — via [Saudisoft](https://localization.saudisoft.com/?p=38442)
- A 2024 academic survey of 382 participants found a strong preference for MSA over colloquial dialects in Arabic mobile game localization — [UAEU research portal](https://research.uaeu.ac.ae/en/publications/arabic-mobile-game-localizations-gamer-profiles-preferences-and-i/)
- **Apple StoreKit Storefront:** Provides the App Store storefront (country). Apple advises against storing storefront information with customer data because it can change at any time. Changing a Sandbox account's App Store country changes the in-app storefront — [Apple Developer: Storefront](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/storefront.md)
- **Google's 2016 checklist:** Switch the UI to RTL, watch for disconnected or reversed letters and for scrambled order when Arabic and Latin text mix, and localize pricing with local currency and rounding — [Android Developers Blog 2016](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2016/11/your-next-growth-market-realizing-the-potential-of-mena.html?hl=en)

### Inferences
- A reasonable architecture is one `ar` string table in MSA, plus a small per-country overlay for live-ops text, event names, promotional copy, pricing and any culturally gated content such as the Saudi music example. Detect country by storefront (iOS) or Play billing country (Android), and fall back to device region, then to `ar`.
- Device locale such as ar-EG vs ar-SA is a weaker signal of the market than the storefront, because many Gulf residents are expatriates and many users run English device language (inference; no source quantifies this).

### Gaps
- I found no public postmortem from a publisher that shipped dialect-specific in-game string variants per Arab country.
- No source documents how major publishers (Supercell, Tencent, Garena) detect country for Arabic content. IP vs storefront vs locale practice is not public.
- Google Play's billing country API and Android per-app language behaviour for Arabic region variants were not researched in depth.

## 4. UA and ad creatives: Gulf vs Egyptian dialect, voiceover, influencers

### Takeaway
Practitioner consensus is to use Khaleeji or Saudi dialect (or MSA) for Gulf gaming ads and not to run Egyptian-dialect creative in the Gulf just because it is widely understood. Egyptian works as a test cell, and for Egypt itself. No public, controlled CTR or CVR comparison by dialect exists for mobile games. The available performance numbers are agency-reported.

### Cited Findings
- Agency guidance favours Khaleeji for gaming creative and MSA for fintech and health. For Saudi Arabia, audiences reportedly prefer MSA over Khaleeji in most verticals, but gaming responds even more strongly to dialect than in the UAE. The agency also claims Arabic-first creative outperforms English 2x on CTR and install rate. All of this is agency self-report and unaudited — [Admiral Media](https://admiral.media/mobile-marketing-agency-dubai/)
- A D2C agency claims translated (not transcreated) Arabic creative sees CTRs 60-80% lower than native-built Arabic creative. The figure is vendor-sourced and from e-commerce, not games — [upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/arabic-creative-strategy-d2c-gcc/)
- Snapchat holds meaningful MENA share and should be budgeted as a primary channel. During Ramadan, daytime CPMs collapse and evening CPMs triple, and the creative tone should shift to family and iftar contexts (agency claim) — [Admiral Media](https://admiral.media/mobile-marketing-agency-dubai/)
- Google MENA research says 44% of women in the UAE play mobile games (2021 data) and recommends featuring regional women gaming creators in creative — [Think with Google: Win Ramadan MENA](https://business.google.com/en-all/think/consumer-insights/win-ramadan-mena-with-2-gaming-audiences/)
- A 2007 advertiser quote calls Egyptian Arabic the lingua franca from the Maghreb to the Gulf (old) — [Asharq Al-Awsat 2007](https://en-archive.aawsat.com/2007/03/21/saudi-advertisements-which-dialect-dominates/)
- **2025 UA benchmarks (Adjust, via Game Growth Advisor):** Saudi CPI +5%, UAE CPI +54%, UAE CPM $3.25 (+55%), UAE IPM 6.49 (+7%), and gaming Day-1 retention of 21% in both KSA and the UAE — [Game Growth Advisor, May 2026](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-05-28-mena-gaming-market-entry-guide-2026/)
- Google notes Saudi Arabia was the second-largest YouTube market globally by views per capita (2016) and recommends YouTube for reach — [Android Developers Blog 2016](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2016/11/your-next-growth-market-realizing-the-potential-of-mena.html?hl=en)

### Inferences
- Test at least three creative cells per major market: Saudi/Khaleeji, MSA and Egyptian. Hold audience, placement and spend fixed, and read CTR, CVR and CPI per country. Egyptian voiceover is likely the right choice only for Egypt-targeted campaigns, and possibly as a pan-Arab fallback.
- With rising UAE CPMs and CPIs (2025), creative efficiency gains from native-dialect transcreation matter more there.

### Gaps
- No measured CTR or CVR by dialect for mobile games was found. The Adjust Tamatem case study (adjust.com) returned HTTP 429 and could not be read. It may contain UA metrics.
- No data on influencer dialect choice (Saudi vs Egyptian creators) or its measured performance.

## 5. Market data per country (2023-2026)

### Takeaway
Saudi Arabia is the clear tier-1 market: 59.1% of MENA-3 games revenue (2024), roughly 51.6% iOS share (Sep 2026), carrier billing on all major networks, and mada. The UAE has the highest ARPU ($84.60, 2024) but a much smaller player base and rising UA costs. Kuwait looks tier-1 on iOS share (~48%) but lacks per-country revenue data. Egypt and Iraq are volume markets: Egypt has the largest player base and the most engagement but an ARPU of $3.39 (2024), and both are about 83-86% Android. Most of the 13 countries (Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Yemen, Jordan, Lebanon, Syria, Palestine) have no public per-country game revenue data.

### Cited Findings
**MENA-3 (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Egypt), Niko Partners. Figures cover all games, not mobile only.**
- Games revenue of $2.15B in 2025 (+7.1%), $2.31B forecast for 2026, and $2.94B by 2030. An earlier forecast put 2025 at about $2.2B and 2029 at $2.78B — [Game Growth Advisor 2026](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-05-28-mena-gaming-market-entry-guide-2026/); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/mena-3-games-market-set-to-reach-28bn-by-2029/); [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/other/item-48190)
- A 2022 Niko forecast of $3.1B and 85.8M players for 2025 overshot actual results — [ResetEra thread of Niko report](https://www.resetera.com/threads/niko-partners-report-big-mena-countries-ksa-uae-egypt-will-hit-85-8m-players-3-1b-in-revenues-by-2025-mena-is-a-a-key-growth-region.537725/)
- **2024 revenue split:** Saudi Arabia 59.1%, UAE 33.7%, Egypt 7.2% — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/mena-3-games-market-set-to-reach-28bn-by-2029/)
- **2024 ARPU:** UAE $84.60 (forecast $100 by 2030), Saudi Arabia $54.89, Egypt $3.39 — [Game Growth Advisor 2026](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-05-28-mena-gaming-market-entry-guide-2026/); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/mena-3-games-market-set-to-reach-28bn-by-2029/)

## 6. Player expectations: Arabic vs English preference, and translation errors players notice

### Takeaway
About 41% of GCC gamers play only, or more readily, games with Arabic localization (Dec 2023 survey). 27.4% might quit a game over poor localization, and about 74-75% say Arab cultural representation matters. Surveys favour MSA over dialect for in-game text, and "partial" localization (text without dubbing) over full Arabic voice-over. The errors players and researchers notice most are technical: disconnected or reversed letters, broken RTL layouts, and mixed Arabic/Latin ordering. After that come literal translation, inconsistent terminology, and missing context.

### Cited Findings
- **Niko Partners / SEF "Speaking the Language: Localization in the MENA region" (1,225 GCC gamers, Dec 2023):**
  - 41% exclusively play, or are more inclined to play, games with Arabic localization. One summary phrases this as "40.9% say Arabic localization is important; the rest are comfortable in English".
  - 27.4% might discontinue a game because of inadequate localization.
  - 74% say Arabic representation in games is very or somewhat important. Arab News reports over 75% for Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Qatar.
  - Sources: [Arab News 2024](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2567682/amp); [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/niko-partners-and-saudi-esports-federation?open=false); [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/analytics/item-44702)
- The SEF country report (updated Mar 2026) repeats the 41% figure and notes that only 3.5% of Steam titles are localized into Arabic — [Game Industry Library / SEF](https://gameindustrylibrary.com/documents/localization-in-the-mena-region-2024/read)
- A 2024 survey of 382 people found a strong preference for MSA over colloquial dialects. Many gamers still play non-localized games for reasons of cost and access — [UAEU](https://research.uaeu.ac.ae/en/publications/arabic-mobile-game-localizations-gamer-profiles-preferences-and-i/)
- A 2022 *Perspectives* study found most Arab gamers prefer "partially localized" games, without Arabic dubbing or re-voiced cutscenes, over fully localized ones — [UAEU: Current trends in localizing video games into Arabic](https://research.uaeu.ac.ae/en/publications/current-trends-in-localizing-video-games-into-arabic-localization/)
- **Error taxonomy (English-to-Arabic game localization):** Orthographic errors such as disconnected letters, inconsistent terminology, word-order problems and collocation errors — [An-Najah University study](https://staff.najah.edu/en/publications/18772/)
- **The Witcher 3 Arabic (2023 study):** The UI was not designed for RTL, which caused layout issues, and taboo visuals were retained — [UAEU: Beyond translation](https://research.uaeu.ac.ae/en/publications/beyond-translation-a-thorough-analysis-of-the-arabic-localisation/)
- **Cyberpunk 2077 Arabic:** A playthrough LQA found at least 500 errors, many caused by translators lacking in-game context — [Saudi Digital Library](https://drepo.sdl.edu.sa/items/a726dbb5-08b3-416b-8f42-0813d3869749/full)
- A 2024 study of 22 games in the Apple "Play in Arabic" sub-category examined technical, linguistic and cultural issues — [Tandfonline 2024](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/23311983.2024.2433314)
- **Engine-level causes:** Unity TextMeshPro does not apply RTL direction or Arabic shaping by default, and fonts may lack Arabic Presentation Forms. Developers report that fixing direction is easy and letter shaping is hard — [Bugnet: Unity Arabic RTL fix](https://bugnet.io/blog/fix-unity-localization-arabic-rtl-text-mirrored-wrong); [itch.io dev thread](https://itch.io/post/12839645)
- Tamatem's founder flagged English fonts that break Arabic letter connections as a common problem (2018) — [Tamatem / Slator](https://tamatem.co/?p=3544)

### Inferences
- The highest-ROI quality gates are technical rendering QA (shaping, RTL, bidi with numbers and Latin names, font coverage), followed by contextual in-game LQA by native players. Dialect choice ranks below both for in-game text.
- Text-only localization captures most of the preference. Full voice-over is lower priority unless the game is narrative-heavy.

### Gaps
- No systematic mining of app-store reviews for Arabic translation complaints was found. Player-complaint evidence is mostly academic or developer-side.
- No per-country breakdown of the Arabic vs English preference, and no data for Egypt or the Levant in the Niko survey, which was GCC only.

## 7. Localization vendors and native LQA resources for Arabic games, and rates

### Takeaway
Specialist options include Tamatem, an Amman-based publisher that localizes in-house. I found no specialist-vendor rate cards for Localsoft, Keywords, Lionbridge, Gameloc, Altagram, LocalizeDirect, Transphere, Saudisoft or Allcorrect. The only price points found are Egypt-based freelancers asking about $0.08 or €0.08 per word ($25-€30 per hour).

### Cited Findings
- Tamatem localizes with in-house native staff and hires freelancers as needed, without LSPs. Its process includes playing the game before translating and avoiding literal translation — [Tamatem / Slator 2018](https://tamatem.co/?p=3544); [TechCrunch 2018](https://techcrunch.com/2018/02/01/tamatem/)
- Vendors publishing Arabic game localization guidance include LocalizeDirect, Transphere (2026 guide), Altagram, Saudisoft (MENA game localization), Allcorrect (Middle East market overview), Alconost and FoxData. None of them publish rates in the material found — [LocalizeDirect](https://www.localizedirect.com/posts/arabic-game-localization); [Transphere](https://www.transphere.com/arabic-game-localization/); [Altagram](https://altagram.com/challenges-in-arabic-localization-altagram/); [Saudisoft](https://localization.saudisoft.com/?p=38442); [Allcorrect](https://allcorrectgames.com/insights/middle-east-game-market-overview/); [Alconost](https://alconost.com/en/blog/hardest-languages-game-localization)

### Inferences
- Budget translation, LQA and voice-over as separate line items. Gulf-native reviewers for the Saudi creative and listing overlays are a separate, likely higher-cost resource than general MSA translators. This is general industry expectation and is not sourced here.

### Gaps
